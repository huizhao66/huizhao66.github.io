Brief Bio

Dr. Zhao is an Associate Professor at the University of North Texas in the Computer Science and Engineering department. She is the PI of the Lab for Accelerated Computer ARChitectures (LARC).

Her research interests include computer architecture and systems, HPC and bio-computing systems. She is currently developing GPU/FPGA/Wafer-Scale Chips to accelerate AI/Machine Learning and genome/DNA/RNA computing.

She received her BS and MSEE degrees from Harbin Institute of Technology and Northeastern University (Boston), and her Ph.D. degree from Pennsylvania State University. She also brings extensive industry experience working as a hardware engineer prior to joining academia.
